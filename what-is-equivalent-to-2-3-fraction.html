What Is Equivalent to 2/3 Fraction?

Equivalent fractions look different but stand for exactly the same value. This page explains what fractions are equivalent to 2/3, how to find them, and how to check your answer.

Understanding the fraction 2/3

2/3 means 2 parts out of 3 equal parts. If a chocolate bar is split into 3 equal pieces and you eat 2, you have eaten 2/3 of the bar.

What are equivalent fractions?

Two fractions are equivalent when they represent the same portion, even though their numbers are different. You make one by multiplying or dividing both the top and bottom numbers by the same whole number.

Some fractions equivalent to 2/3 include: 4/6, 6/9, 8/12.

How to find equivalent fractions, step by step

Step 1: Start with 2/3.

Step 2: Pick any whole number, for example 2.

Step 3: Multiply the top and the bottom by it: 2 × 2 = 4, and 3 × 2 = 6. So one equivalent fraction is 4/6.

You can repeat this with any whole number, so there are infinitely many equivalent fractions.

Why equivalent fractions are useful

Simplifying: A larger equivalent fraction can often be divided back down to 2/3.

Comparing: Two fractions are easier to compare once they share a denominator.

Adding and subtracting: Equivalent fractions let you rewrite fractions with a common denominator before you add or subtract them.

2/3 as a decimal and a percent

Decimal: 2 ÷ 3 = 0.666667. Percent: 0.666667 × 100 = 66.67%. Every fraction equivalent to 2/3 has the same decimal and percent.

How to check two fractions are equivalent

Cross-multiply. For example, is 6/9 equivalent to 2/3? Check 2 × 9 = 18 and 3 × 6 = 18. The two products are equal, so yes, they are equivalent.

Common mistakes

Multiplying only one part. You must multiply both the numerator and the denominator by the same number.

Using different multipliers. If the top is multiplied by 2 and the bottom by 3, the value changes.

Real-life examples

Cooking: A recipe needing 2/3 cup can also be measured as 4/6 cup or 6/9 cup.

Time: 2/3 of an hour is 60 × 2/3 = 40.00 minutes, approximately.

Progress: Completing 2/3 of a project means about 66.67% of it is done.
