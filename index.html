How to use the calculator

Tap the buttons or type the numbers and operators. For trigonometry, tap sin, cos or tan first, then the angle in degrees, then close the bracket. For a power, use the x² and x³ buttons or type **. Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to use the calculator?

No. Open the page, type your calculation and press equals.

Are sin, cos and tan in degrees or radians?

In degrees, which matches most school work. For example, sin(30) gives 0.5.

Can I convert a fraction to a percent here?

Yes. Divide the fraction, then multiply by 100. For example, 1 ÷ 4 × 100 = 25%. The Percent and Decimal guides show the same steps for 12 common fractions.
