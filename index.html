How to use the calculator

Tap the buttons, or click the display and type the whole sum. The answer appears live below the display as you type; press Enter or = for the final answer.

For trigonometry, tap sin, cos or tan first, then the angle, then close the bracket. Angles are in degrees by default; press RAD first if your angle is in radians. The inverse buttons sin⁻¹, cos⁻¹ and tan⁻¹ give you the angle back, in the same mode.

For a power, use the x² and x³ buttons or type **. For a percentage, type the number followed by %. For a deeper explanation of sine, cosine and tangent, see Khan Academy's trigonometry course.

Worked examples

Four sums worked out in full, so you can see the method before you try your own.

sin(30) = 0.5. Tap sin, type 30, close the bracket and press =. The angle is 30 degrees, and the sine of 30 degrees is 0.5.

15% of 80 = 12. Change 15% to 0.15, then multiply: 0.15 × 80 = 12. Or type 15%*80 into the calculator.

√144 = 12. 12 × 12 = 144, so the square root of 144 is 12.

1/4 as a percent = 25%. Divide 1 by 4 to get 0.25, then multiply by 100. The fraction to percent tool below shows each step.

Common mistakes

Degrees and radians mixed up. If sin(90) gives 0.89 instead of 1, you are in radians. Press DEG first.

Forgetting the × 100 step. 0.25 is the decimal. The percent is 0.25 × 100 = 25%.

Dividing the wrong way round. For 1/4, divide 1 by 4, not 4 by 1.

A missing bracket. Type sin(30) with both brackets. A missing bracket gives Error.

The % key on its own. 50% alone means 0.5. For 15% of 80, type 15%*80, not 15% alone.

Frequently asked questions

Does the calculator work in degrees and radians?

Yes. Angles are in degrees by default. Press RAD before you type an angle in radians. The DEG and RAD buttons sit above the keys, and sin⁻¹, cos⁻¹ and tan⁻¹ give the angle back in the same mode.

How do I turn a fraction into a percent?

Divide the top number by the bottom number to get the decimal, then multiply by 100. For example, 1/4 = 0.25, and 0.25 × 100 = 25%. The fraction to percent tool above shows each step.

What fractions are equivalent to 3/4?

Any fraction made by multiplying the top and bottom of 3/4 by the same number: 6/8, 9/12, 12/16 and 15/20. The equivalent fractions tool above makes the whole family for any fraction.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The calculator and the tools run in any modern browser, on a phone, a tablet or a computer, and on school Chromebooks.

Is my calculation private?

Yes. The maths runs in your browser. What you type is not sent to a server for the calculation, and no account is needed.

Can I type a calculation instead of tapping the buttons?

Yes. Click the display and type the whole sum, for example sin(30). The answer shows live below the display as you type, and pressing Enter gives the final answer.

Does the calculator remember my calculations?

Yes, the last 10 calculations are saved in your browser and are still there when you come back. Nothing is sent to a server.

What are the M+, MR and Ans buttons for?

M+ adds the number on screen to memory, MR brings it back, MC clears it, and Ans puts your last answer into the next calculation. Memory stays in your browser only.
