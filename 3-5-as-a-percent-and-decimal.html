3/5 as a Percent and Decimal

Fractions, decimals and percentages are three ways of writing the same value. This page shows, step by step, how to turn 3/5 into a decimal and then into a percent, with a few everyday examples so the method sticks.

Understanding the fraction 3/5

3/5 means 3 part out of 5 equal parts of a whole. The top number is the numerator, the part you have. The bottom number is the denominator, the total number of equal parts.

For example, if a pizza is cut into 5 equal slices and you eat 3 slices, you have eaten 3/5 of the pizza.

How to convert 3/5 into a decimal

Divide the numerator by the denominator.

Step 1: Write it as division: 3 ÷ 5

Step 2: Work it out: 3 ÷ 5 = 0.6

So, 3/5 as a decimal is 0.6.

How to convert 3/5 into a percent

Take the decimal and multiply it by 100.

Step 1: Start with the decimal: 0.6

Step 2: Multiply by 100: 0.6 × 100 = 60%

So, 3/5 as a percent is 60%.

Why this conversion matters

Cooking and baking

If a recipe asks for 3/5 cup of sugar, that is the same as 0.6 cup, or 60% of one cup.

Time

3/5 of one hour is 60 × 3/5 = 36 minutes.

Money and shopping

If you spend 3/5 of your budget, you have spent 60% of your total money. A discount of the same size means you save that share of the price.

Another way to get the percent

Multiply the fraction by 100 directly: (3/5) × 100 = 300/5 = 60%.

Common mistakes

Dividing the wrong way round. Always divide the numerator by the denominator, not the other way round.

Forgetting the × 100 step. The decimal alone is not the percent. Multiplying by 100 is what turns it into a percent.

Real-life examples

Food: Eating 3 slices from 5 slices means you ate 60% of the pizza.

School work: Finishing 3 out of 5 assignments means you finished 60% of the work.

Shopping: A discount equal to 3/5 of the price saves you 60% of what you would have paid.
