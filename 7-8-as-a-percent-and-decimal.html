7/8 as a Percent and Decimal

Fractions, decimals and percentages are three ways of writing the same value. This page shows, step by step, how to turn 7/8 into a decimal and then into a percent, with a few everyday examples so the method sticks.

Understanding the fraction 7/8

7/8 means 7 part out of 8 equal parts of a whole. The top number is the numerator, the part you have. The bottom number is the denominator, the total number of equal parts.

For example, if a pizza is cut into 8 equal slices and you eat 7 slices, you have eaten 7/8 of the pizza.

How to convert 7/8 into a decimal

Divide the numerator by the denominator.

Step 1: Write it as division: 7 ÷ 8

Step 2: Work it out: 7 ÷ 8 = 0.875

So, 7/8 as a decimal is 0.875.

How to convert 7/8 into a percent

Take the decimal and multiply it by 100.

Step 1: Start with the decimal: 0.875

Step 2: Multiply by 100: 0.875 × 100 = 87.50%

So, 7/8 as a percent is 87.50%.

Why this conversion matters

Cooking and baking

If a recipe asks for 7/8 cup of sugar, that is the same as 0.875 cup, or 87.50% of one cup.

Time

7/8 of one hour is 60 × 7/8 = 52.5 minutes.

Money and shopping

If you spend 7/8 of your budget, you have spent 87.50% of your total money. A discount of the same size means you save that share of the price.

Another way to get the percent

Multiply the fraction by 100 directly: (7/8) × 100 = 700/8 = 87.50%.

Common mistakes

Dividing the wrong way round. Always divide the numerator by the denominator, not the other way round.

Forgetting the × 100 step. The decimal alone is not the percent. Multiplying by 100 is what turns it into a percent.

Real-life examples

Food: Eating 7 slices from 8 slices means you ate 87.50% of the pizza.

School work: Finishing 7 out of 8 assignments means you finished 87.50% of the work.

Shopping: A discount equal to 7/8 of the price saves you 87.50% of what you would have paid.
