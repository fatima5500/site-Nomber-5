5/6 as a Percent and Decimal

Fractions, decimals and percentages are three ways of writing the same value. This page shows, step by step, how to turn 5/6 into a decimal and then into a percent, with a few everyday examples so the method sticks.

Understanding the fraction 5/6

5/6 means 5 part out of 6 equal parts of a whole. The top number is the numerator, the part you have. The bottom number is the denominator, the total number of equal parts.

For example, if a pizza is cut into 6 equal slices and you eat 5 slices, you have eaten 5/6 of the pizza.

How to convert 5/6 into a decimal

Divide the numerator by the denominator.

Step 1: Write it as division: 5 ÷ 6

Step 2: Work it out: 5 ÷ 6 = 0.833333

So, 5/6 as a decimal is 0.833333.

How to convert 5/6 into a percent

Take the decimal and multiply it by 100.

Step 1: Start with the decimal: 0.833333

Step 2: Multiply by 100: 0.833333 × 100 = 83.33%

So, 5/6 as a percent is 83.33%.

Why this conversion matters

Cooking and baking

If a recipe asks for 5/6 cup of sugar, that is the same as 0.833333 cup, or 83.33% of one cup.

Time

5/6 of one hour is 60 × 5/6 = 50 minutes.

Money and shopping

If you spend 5/6 of your budget, you have spent 83.33% of your total money. A discount of the same size means you save that share of the price.

Another way to get the percent

Multiply the fraction by 100 directly: (5/6) × 100 = 500/6 = 83.33%.

Common mistakes

Dividing the wrong way round. Always divide the numerator by the denominator, not the other way round.

Forgetting the × 100 step. The decimal alone is not the percent. Multiplying by 100 is what turns it into a percent.

Real-life examples

Food: Eating 5 slices from 6 slices means you ate 83.33% of the pizza.

School work: Finishing 5 out of 6 assignments means you finished 83.33% of the work.

Shopping: A discount equal to 5/6 of the price saves you 83.33% of what you would have paid.
