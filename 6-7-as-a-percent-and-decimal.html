6/7 as a Percent and Decimal

Fractions, decimals and percentages are three ways of writing the same value. This page shows, step by step, how to turn 6/7 into a decimal and then into a percent, with a few everyday examples so the method sticks.

Understanding the fraction 6/7

6/7 means 6 part out of 7 equal parts of a whole. The top number is the numerator, the part you have. The bottom number is the denominator, the total number of equal parts.

For example, if a pizza is cut into 7 equal slices and you eat 6 slices, you have eaten 6/7 of the pizza.

How to convert 6/7 into a decimal

Divide the numerator by the denominator.

Step 1: Write it as division: 6 ÷ 7

Step 2: Work it out: 6 ÷ 7 = 0.857143

So, 6/7 as a decimal is 0.857143.

How to convert 6/7 into a percent

Take the decimal and multiply it by 100.

Step 1: Start with the decimal: 0.857143

Step 2: Multiply by 100: 0.857143 × 100 = 85.71%

So, 6/7 as a percent is 85.71%.

Why this conversion matters

Cooking and baking

If a recipe asks for 6/7 cup of sugar, that is the same as 0.857143 cup, or 85.71% of one cup.

Time

6/7 of one hour is 60 × 6/7 = 51.43 minutes.

Money and shopping

If you spend 6/7 of your budget, you have spent 85.71% of your total money. A discount of the same size means you save that share of the price.

Another way to get the percent

Multiply the fraction by 100 directly: (6/7) × 100 = 600/7 = 85.71%.

Common mistakes

Dividing the wrong way round. Always divide the numerator by the denominator, not the other way round.

Forgetting the × 100 step. The decimal alone is not the percent. Multiplying by 100 is what turns it into a percent.

Real-life examples

Food: Eating 6 slices from 7 slices means you ate 85.71% of the pizza.

School work: Finishing 6 out of 7 assignments means you finished 85.71% of the work.

Shopping: A discount equal to 6/7 of the price saves you 85.71% of what you would have paid.
